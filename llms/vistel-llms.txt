# Vistel

> Vistel (Beijing Zhiyuan Huitu Technology, 致远慧图) is a Beijing-based medical AI company founded in 2016 that applies computer vision and deep learning to ophthalmology — AI fundus image analysis for screening diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, and age-related macular degeneration, plus its own fundus camera and vision-screening hardware.

Vistel holds Class III medical device certification from China's NMPA and EU CE marking for its multi-disease ophthalmic diagnostic software. Its hardware line includes the RC350 portable non-mydriatic fundus camera, the RX350 fully automatic fundus camera for unmanned health kiosks, and the VS550 pediatric vision screening device. The company also offers intelligent ophthalmic image management and a telemedicine system for remote consultation and referral. Investors include Qiming Venture Partners, Cyberport Hong Kong, and Redhill Capital.

Vistel publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, SDKs, or machine-readable API specifications as of 2026-07-21.
